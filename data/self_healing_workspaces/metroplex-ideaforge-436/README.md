# Elder-Care Safety & Support Companion

Sarah is 32. She lives with her 68-year-old mother, who has lived with a mood and personality condition for most of Sarah's life. Most nights are quiet, but a few nights a month her mother yells, throws things, or says something that frightens Sarah enough to lock herself in her bedroom. Sarah feels guilty about leaving — her mother has nowhere else to go — but she also knows she can't keep absorbing this alone. She needs a calm, private way to document what happened, see her own patterns, and know what number to call when a night gets bad enough.

This is what the Elder-Care Safety & Support Companion does. When Sarah types `"Mom screamed at me and threw the remote tonight"`, the agent records a structured incident, anchored at word boundaries so `"argumentative"` and `"incidental"` don't pollute her log. When she types `"I didn't have an argument with Mom"`, the agent honors the negation, logs a silent-drop audit row, and tells her plainly that nothing was filed as an incident. When she types `"I'm scared to come home"`, the agent surfaces the National Domestic Violence Hotline, the Adult Protective Services Locator, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, and the Family Caregiver Alliance — even when no recognizable incident token appeared in her message. And when she says `"I feel guilty about seeking help"`, the agent validates that feeling explicitly before reminding her that reaching out is not abandonment.

Invocation, from a CCOS shell, is `claude --agent eldercare` for an interactive session, or `mission-cli run eldercare --message "tell me what happened"` for a single message. The agent reads `agent.yaml` for its model and telegram bot env-var configuration. T1 deliberately stays narrow: there is no web frontend, no voice synthesis, no calendar integration, no external HTTP calls, no benefits or billing workflow, no cross-session memory, and no multi-user partitioning — Sarah's data stays on Sarah's CCOS host.

Deployment is simple. Drop the agent directory into a CCOS-registered agent root and set `ELDERCARE_BOT_TOKEN` in the local environment if you want the Telegram entry point alongside the CLI one. Incident logs land in `data/incidents.jsonl`; silent-drop audit rows land in `data/silent_drops.jsonl`. Every confirmation Sarah sees has already been read back from disk — the agent never says "logged" without verifying the write — and every silent drop is reviewable so she has a complete picture of what the agent did and didn't act on.
